QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

Is it really a one-time purchase?

Yes. Pay $4.99 once and Pro is yours, including for your family through Family Sharing. There's no subscription version and we're not planning one.

What can I do without paying?

Most of it. All eleven calculators, unlimited rooms and projects, every guide, and the totaled shopping list are free. Pro adds FixLens, Home Records, exports, and extra houses.

Does it work offline?

Yes, all of it. Measure in the basement, the attic, or the far corner of the yard — no signal needed.

Where does my data live?

On your phone, and that's it. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no ads. Apple's privacy label for the app reads "Data Not Collected."

What's Smart Vision, and is it private?

Smart Vision is an optional AI upgrade for FixLens on newer iPhones. It's a one-time download (about 1 GB) that runs entirely on your device — it reads tricky appliance displays the basic camera reader can't. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is analyzed in a cloud, and it works in airplane mode. It's in beta and completely optional.

What about Android?

iPhone only for now. If you'd use it on Android, tell us at features@castlewise.app — we keep count, and enough requests will get it built.
