How it works

Four jobs, one app.

Most home apps are a reminder list with a subscription. This one actually knows things.

01

Write it down once

Snap the data plate, save the paint code, log the filter size. Starter packs mean you never face a blank form.

02

What's inside

Everything, in one place.

Built over a year of nights and weekends, then rebuilt from beta feedback.

Home records

Twelve categories covering appliances, HVAC, water, paint, flooring, safety, exterior, service history, and tests and inspections like radon and water quality. Photos, exports for insurance, and full search.

Reminders that fit a house

Editable seasonal checklists, plus home dates for filter changes, HVAC service, smoke detector batteries, septic pumping, insurance renewals, and holiday lights. Set how far ahead you want the nudge.

Guides and calculators

86 full projects, each calling out the mistake first-timers make. 1,600 more you can vote into existence. Eleven calculators, a know-how library, and a 100-term trade dictionary in plain words.

Electrical panel map

Label every breaker in a grid that looks like your panel door. It knows your panel brand and tells you the exact breaker to buy, because Square D QO and Homeline are not interchangeable. Sub-panels included.

FixLens

267 verified error codes across 53 brands. 120 problem playbooks from squeaky floors to sewage smells. Runs on your phone. When it doesn't know, it says so instead of guessing.

Your house, your way

Name it whatever you call it. Give every room its own icon and color from 54 symbols and 12 shades. The app builds your rooms during setup, then gets out of the way.
