TESTFLIGHT, EXPLAINED IN 60 SECONDS.

TestFlight is Apple's official app for testing apps before they launch — made by Apple, free, safe, and gone-in-two-taps when the beta ends.

  1. Download TestFlight

    App Store → search "TestFlight" (blue propeller icon, by Apple) → Get. Or tap: TestFlight on the App Store.

  2. Open your invite

    When our invite email arrives, open it ON YOUR iPHONE and tap "View in TestFlight" → "Accept" → "Install." CastleWise appears on your home screen like any app — with a small orange dot by its name, TestFlight's way of saying "beta."

  3. Sending feedback is built in

    Take a screenshot inside CastleWise, tap the thumbnail, and choose "Share Beta Feedback" — it comes straight to us with the screen attached. Or just email features@castlewise.app like a normal person.

  4. Updates arrive by themselves

    TestFlight notifies you when a new build lands. Open TestFlight → Update. Beta builds expire after 90 days, so updating now and then keeps you in the club.

QUICK QUESTIONS.

Does the beta cost anything?

No. Beta builds have everything unlocked, free, for the whole test. When the app launches, it'll be free with a one-time $4.99 Pro upgrade — never a subscription.

Is TestFlight safe?

It's Apple's own testing app — the official, sanctioned way every iPhone app gets tested before launch. Deleting it and the beta removes everything.

What happens to my info?

CastleWise collects nothing — no account, no cloud, no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your phone. Photos are analyzed on-device and never uploaded. Privacy policy.

What iPhone do I need?

Anything running iOS 18 or newer. The optional Smart Vision AI feature wants a recent model, but everything else runs on years-old phones happily.

When does it launch for real?

September. Beta testers get there first — and shape what "there" looks like.
