CASTLEWISE

PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE JULY 2026 · CASTLEWISE FOR iOS

The short version: CastleWise collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your device. We couldn't look at your information if we wanted to — there's no server for it to go to.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. CastleWise has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting services, and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests to us — it works entirely offline.

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES

Everything you enter — rooms, projects, calculator results, tool lists, and Home Records entries — is stored locally on your device, and nowhere else. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. If your device is backed up (for example with iCloud device backup), your CastleWise data is included in that backup under the terms of Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it.

PURCHASES

CastleWise Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, email address, or payment details. Apple provides us only anonymous, aggregated sales reporting.

EMAIL

If you choose to email us at features@castlewise.app, we'll have your email address because you sent it — we use it only to reply, and we don't add it to any list.

CHILDREN

CastleWise collects no data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES

If our practices ever change, we'll update this page and the app's App Store privacy label before the change takes effect.
