# Ink Run — Volume 1 story panels

Hero: **Nozomi** (望, "hope"). Chasers: the **Smudge Crew**, a rooftop gang in ink-black
jackets, and the **Shadow**, a kaiju made of ink and screentone that no one has seen whole.
Working names; easy to change. Captions are short on purpose: they sit on comic-panel cards
for about two seconds each. SFX words go on the art, not in the caption.

## How transitions play

A chapter change happens every 1,200 m while the run keeps going. The game shows three
panels in a row at the top of the screen, one every 0.7 s, over the live run, then the
CHAPTER card. The run never pauses. If the player crashes mid-sequence, the panels cut
instantly to the crash.

| Panel | Caption | Art note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dawn again. The city looks the same. | Alley opens onto a stairwell, red sun rising |

## Death panels (crash → "To Be Continued...?")

Each crash plays a three-panel strip in about one second before the revive page: approach,
impact, aftermath. The impact panel holds the SFX word. The aftermath panel becomes Panel 1
of the revive page, so the page feels like it continues the strip.

| Obstacle | Panel 1 (approach) | Panel 2 (impact, SFX) | Panel 3 (aftermath) → revive page line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barrier | Nozomi's sneaker catches the top rail | Face-first over the barrier. WHAM! | Flat on the roof, goggles knocked sideways. "The rail was higher than it looked." |
| Sign | The sign board fills the frame, STOP in red | Clotheslined, feet still running in the air. ガン! (GAN) | Sitting against the post, stars circling. "He read the sign. Too late." |
| Train | Headlight flare, the horn as a big word | Impact, hoodie strings flying, coins scattering. ドン! (DON) | Spread-eagle on the track, one coin still spinning. "The Nozomi doesn't stop for anyone." (the train is named after him, he hates that) |
| Gap | Toes over the edge, nothing below | Falling, arms windmilling, the Crew looking down from above. ヒュー (whoosh) | Caught on a laundry line two floors down. "Lucky. This time." |

The revive page's Panel 2 is always the chaser: "Somewhere behind him, the Crew is laughing.
Above them, the Shadow gets closer." Panel 3 is the distance line.

## Art plan for the death strips

Each strip is generated as one 16:9 image with three vertical panels in the house style
(ink, screentone, one red accent). To animate: cut the three panels apart, show them in
sequence with a hard cut and a 0.05 s white flash, slide panel 2 in 20 px from the right on
impact, shake the screen on the SFX, hold panel 3, then slide the revive page up over it.
No tweening inside a panel; the cuts are the animation, like a manga page turn.
