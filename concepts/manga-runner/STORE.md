## Description

Nozomi took something from the Smudge Crew. Now the whole city is a chase.

Ink Run is an endless runner drawn like a printed manga: black ink, screentone shadows,
and one bright color that changes with every chapter. Red rooftops. Blue harbor. Purple
alleys at night.

Swipe to change lanes. Swipe up to jump. Swipe down to slide. That's all you need.

Crash, and the page breaks into panels: TO BE CONTINUED...? Keep running or turn the page.

- Three chapters that loop, each with its own color and its own obstacles
- Sound-effect words that pop when you jump, dash, and crash. WHAM!
- Coin magnet and speed dash power-ups
- Daily missions and outfits for Nozomi
- A story that unfolds between chapters, one volume at a time
- Short runs. One thumb. Plays in portrait.

Volume 1 is just the beginning. The Shadow is still out there.
